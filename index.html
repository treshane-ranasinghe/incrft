The Atelier

Made by hand,
one pour at a time

Our studio sits in a converted cooperage where every candle is still poured, cured and finished by hand. We blend a clean coconut-soy wax, perfume it with oils from Grasse, and leave each batch to cure for fourteen days before it's ready.

  • i.

    Clean-burning wax

    Coconut and soy, free from paraffin and synthetic dyes.

  • ii.

    Perfumery-grade oils

    Up to 12% fragrance load for an even throw that fills the room.

  • iii.

    Vessels for keeping

    Mouth-blown glass and stoneware worth keeping once the candle is finished.

The Ritual

Care for your candle

  1. I

    The first burn

    Let the wax melt all the way to the edge on the first light, usually two to three hours. This sets its memory and prevents tunnelling.

  2. II

    Trim the wick

    Trim to 5mm before each lighting for a calm, clean flame and no soot on the glass.

  3. III

    Extinguish gently

    Use a snuffer or dip the wick into the melted wax. This keeps the smoke away and the fragrance intact.

  4. IV

    Keep the vessel

    Once finished, warm the glass, lift out the remaining wax and keep the vessel for flowers, brushes or treasures.
